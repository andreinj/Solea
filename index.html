The Challenge

Most AV contractors treat your parish
like a conference room.

High-liturgical churches face AV challenges that a general contractor simply cannot understand — because they have never stood at the kliros, watched a deacon move through the nave, or heard what chant sounds like when a space is properly tuned for it.

Solea AV was built from the inside out — by an Orthodox Christian and degreed audio engineer who has served in these spaces and knows exactly what they need.

Microphones that disrupt the visual reverence of the space

Ugly, visible mics at the kliros and altar that pull the eye away from the iconostasis.

Clergy and chanters who cannot be heard clearly

Poor vocal clarity from improperly tuned systems that were never designed for chant.

Families in the narthex who can't follow the service

No audio coverage in the narthex, cry rooms, or parish halls where young families gather.

Homebound parishioners cut off from parish life

No livestreaming capability despite growing demand from the elderly and distant members of the parish.

Systems too complicated for volunteers to operate

Overcomplicated setups that nobody in the parish can reliably run from Sunday to Sunday.

What We Do

Liturgical AV Solutions

Every service is designed with the worship space in mind — technically rigorous, visually discreet, and acoustically appropriate for sacred sound.

Sound Reinforcement

Clergy microphones, choir coverage, narthex and cry room speaker systems. Tuned for the acoustics of chant, not speech in a conference room.

Wireless Microphone Systems

Proper wireless packs so the deacon doesn't hand off a microphone mid-service. Frequency-coordinated systems that work in even the most challenging RF environments.

Livestreaming

Camera, encoder, and streaming setup for YouTube and Facebook. Keep your homebound, elderly, and distant parishioners connected to every service.

System Design & Consulting

If your parish is hiring a general contractor, I can spec the system properly so it gets built right the first time. Proposals, equipment lists, and signal flow diagrams.

Parish Hall & Classroom AV

Projectors, screens, and basic audio for parish schools, fellowship halls, and multi-purpose spaces. Clean setups that volunteers can actually use.

Hearing Loop Systems

Induction loop systems for hearing-impaired parishioners. Invisible, effective, and a genuine act of welcome for every member of the community.

The Process

From conversation to commissioning.

01

Site Survey

We walk the space together — nave, narthex, cry room, parish hall. I listen to the acoustics, document what's there, and understand the flow of your services.

02

Proposal

A clear scope of work, equipment list, timeline, and pricing — no surprises. Designed to be presented to a parish council or priest with confidence.

03

Installation
