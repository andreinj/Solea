like a conference room.

Most AV contractors have never stood at the Cantors Stand, followed a deacon through the nave, or heard what chant sounds like in a space that's been properly tuned for it. That gap shows in their work.

Solea AV was built differently — by people who have worshipped in these spaces, served in them, and spent years working in professional audio at the highest level. We know what the liturgy demands because we live it.

How We Can Help

AV Solutions for Sacred Spaces

Every solution is designed with the worship space in mind. Technically precise, visually discreet, and acoustically appropriate for sacred sound.

Sound Reinforcement

Clergy microphones, choir coverage, narthex and cry room speaker systems. Tuned for the acoustics of chant, not speech in a conference room.

Wireless Microphone Systems

Proper wireless packs so the deacon doesn't hand off a microphone mid-service. Frequency-coordinated systems that work in even the most challenging RF environments.

Livestreaming

Custom broadcast and streaming setups designed for liturgical worship. We help you reach your faithful on any device or platform, keeping your entire community united in prayer.

System Design & Consulting

If your parish is hiring a general contractor, we can spec the system properly so it gets built right the first time. Proposals, equipment lists, and signal flow diagrams.

Parish Hall & Classroom AV

Projectors, screens, and basic audio for parish schools, fellowship halls, and multi-purpose spaces. Clean setups that volunteers can actually use.

Hearing Loop Systems

Induction loop systems for hearing-impaired parishioners. Invisible, effective, and a genuine act of welcome for every member of the community.

How It Works

A straightforward process built around
your parish's needs and schedule.

01

Consultation

01

A complementary consultation to discuss your sanctuary and goals. No strings attached.

It starts with a conversation. Tell us about your space, your challenges, and what you are hoping to achieve. We will ask the right questions and give you an honest assessment of what is possible and what it might cost before any commitment is made.

02

Site Survey

02

A thorough walk-through of your space, acoustics, and existing setup.

We visit your parish, walk the full space, and assess everything. Acoustics, existing equipment, cable pathways, liturgical flow, and the specific needs of your tradition. You will receive a written findings summary with photos.

03

Proposal

03

A detailed written proposal — no hidden fees, no surprises.

We deliver a detailed written proposal covering the recommended system design, equipment list, project scope, timeline, and itemized pricing. No hidden fees, no surprises. We present it in person or over a call and answer every question before you decide.

04

Installation

04

Clean, respectful work scheduled around your liturgical calendar.

We schedule work around your liturgical calendar, weekdays, off-hours, or between services. We arrive on time, work cleanly, and treat your space with the respect it deserves. Most installations are completed in one to three days.

05

Handoff & Support

05

One switch. Full documentation. Ongoing support when you need it.

We design every system to be simple by intention. When the job is done, turning everything on should be a single switch — no trained operator required. We walk your team through exactly that before we leave, provide full documentation of everything installed, and remain available for questions and support after the job is done.
