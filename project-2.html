Project

Holy Trinity Cathedral

Overview

A comprehensive livestreaming system built around a multi-camera setup with a dedicated audio feed, designed to bring the full liturgical experience to remote parishioners without any visual disruption inside the cathedral.

We integrated PTZ cameras at positions chosen to capture the key liturgical moments — the altar, the ambon, and the choir loft — while keeping hardware completely hidden from the congregation's line of sight. A separate broadcast audio mix was built from the existing sound system, optimized for clarity on compressed streaming platforms.

Scope
  • Multi-camera PTZ system (3 cameras)
  • Dedicated broadcast audio mix
  • Video switching and encoding hardware
  • YouTube / Facebook Live integration
  • Operator training and scene presets
Parish Type

Orthodox (OCA)
